Privacy Policy

City Grid citizen app (in.citygrid.citizen) · operated independently
Effective 12 November 2026 · Last updated 12 November 2026

Our privacy design, honestly: City Grid is an independent civic and city-life app — not a municipal corporation portal. Guests can browse the map; reports and My Reports need an account. Public pins show an anonymous id (PW-…), not your email or name. Photos and GPS you submit are uploaded for the shared map and civic briefs. On-device checks may run on your phone before submit. We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

3. How we use information

  • Accept, store, and display civic reports and parking listings.
  • Power the live map and trip-related warnings.
  • Sync My Reports when signed in.
  • Send report confirmation email when configured.
  • Show city weather context where offered.
  • Build independent civic briefs from report data (not a government system of record).
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Service.
  • Run on-device photo/heuristic checks before submit (not a separate commercial AI API for that step in current versions).

7. Account deletion

Email citygridhelp@gmail.com from your registered address. Subject: Delete my City Grid account. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

No in-app delete-one-report while keeping the account yet — email us to request review of a specific item.

8. Data retention

Reports, listings, and photos are kept while useful for the map and status tracking. Account data remains while the account is active, then follows Section 7 on deletion. Limited security logs may be retained as needed.

9. Security

HTTPS in transit; auth passwords handled by the auth provider (not plain text in the app); provider access controls for stored data. No method is 100% secure.

10. Your choices & rights

  • Revoke permissions in Android settings.
  • Delete the app from your device.
  • Request account deletion (Section 7).
  • Update email in-app (verification required).
  • Contact us to access, correct, or delete data, including under applicable Indian law (DPDP) where it applies.

11. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.
